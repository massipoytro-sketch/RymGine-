# DeSiaVe Future Development & Roadmap Ideas

1. **Multiplayer Guilds & Clan Wars**: Cooperative quest completions and shared treasury pools.
2. **NFT & Seasonal Badge System**: On-chain or verified collectible trophies for completing high-difficulty milestones.
3. **Mini Arcade Game Integrations**: Native HTML5 web mini-games where score translates directly to XP and coins.
4. **Push Notifications**: Streak reminder notifications and new high-reward bounty alerts.
5. **Dark / Light Dynamic Theme Skins**: Custom unlockable cyberpunk, fantasy gold, and retro neon visual themes.
